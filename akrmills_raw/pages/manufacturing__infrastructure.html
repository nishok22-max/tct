Seven Production & Testing Stages

Explore our complete plant infrastructure. Every machine is calibrated for minimal fibre stress, high elongation, and lot-to-lot consistency.

STAGE 01

India & Germany

LMW & Trützschler Full Line

Equipment OverviewIndia & Germany

Blowroom Line

LMW & Trützschler Full Line

Automated bale opening and multi-stage cleaning lines from Trützschler and LMW. Gently separates raw cotton and synthetic staple fibres while extracting foreign matter, trash, seed coats, and micro-dust without fibre degradation.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Gentle tuft opening preserving fibre length
Continuous electronic contamination extraction
Uniform blowroom chute feed regularity
STAGE 02

Germany & India

Trützschler & LMW High-Production Cards

Equipment OverviewGermany & India

Carding Machine

Trützschler & LMW High-Production Cards

Precision carding frames from Trützschler and LMW individualize tangled fibre clumps into single parallel fibres. High-density wire flats systematically remove neps, short fibres, and seed coat fragments to form an ultra-consistent sliver.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Active nep extraction and elimination
Precision stationary and revolving flats
Integrated autolevelling for weight consistency
STAGE 03

India & Switzerland

LMW & Rieter Autoleveller Draw Frames

Equipment OverviewIndia & Switzerland

Draw Frame

LMW & Rieter Autoleveller Draw Frames

High-speed draw frames from Rieter and LMW combine multiple card slivers, double-drafting them to parallelize fibres and achieve perfect blend homogeneity for 100% Cotton, PC, and CP/CVC yarns.

Key Performance Capabilities:

State-of-the-art digital autolevelling
Hook straightening and fibre alignment
Guaranteed sliver CV% < 0.6%
STAGE 04

India

LMW Speed Frames

Equipment OverviewIndia

Roving Frame

LMW Speed Frames

LMW speed frames gently draft the drawn sliver into a slender, uniform roving package with controlled helical twist. Roving packages are smoothly transported on creels to ring frames to prevent accidental drafting.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Computerized 4-roller drafting system
Tension-controlled bobbin build
Minimal roving count variation
STAGE 05

India

LMW Ring Spinning Fleet

Equipment OverviewIndia

Ring Spinning Frame

LMW Ring Spinning Fleet

Our modern LMW ring spinning frames deliver the final draft and twist to produce ring-spun yarn in counts from Ne 16s to Ne 60s. Precision spindles and inverter-driven speed curves ensure consistent tension, minimal end breaks, and high yarn strength.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Spindle speeds optimized for low hairiness
Electronic cop-forming & auto-doffing
Consistent twist multiplier across all spindles
STAGE 06

Switzerland & Japan

Saurer & Muratec Precision Autoconers

Equipment OverviewSwitzerland & Japan

Autoconer

Saurer & Muratec Precision Autoconers

Saurer and Muratec automatic winding machines transfer yarn from ring bobbins onto precision export cones. Integrated electronic yarn clearers detect and excise thick places, thin places, and neps, replacing them with invisible thermopneumatic splices.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Optical and capacitive digital yarn clearers
High-tenacity knotless thermopneumatic splices
Uniform cone density for warping and knitting
STAGE 07

Benchmarked to Uster Standards

Climate-Controlled Quality Laboratory

Equipment OverviewBenchmarked to Uster Standards

Yarn Testing Lab

Climate-Controlled Quality Laboratory

Our dedicated on-site testing facility verifies every production lot under controlled temperature and relative humidity. Equipped with high-precision testing apparatus to measure count variation, tensile tenacity, elongation, mass evenness, and hairiness against global Uster statistics.

Key Performance Capabilities:

Constant atmospheric conditioning (20°C, 65% RH)
Lot-by-lot Uster evenness and imperfection testing
Comprehensive digital test certificates issued with consignments
