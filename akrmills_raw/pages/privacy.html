Privacy Policy

At AKR Spinning Mills, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparent handling of your business enquiries.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by you when submitting business enquiries, sample requests, or communications with our team. This includes your name, company name, email address, and specific yarn requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected details exclusively for legitimate B2B operations:

  • Responding to price enquiries and count availability requests.
  • Processing sample shipments and specification sheets.
  • Fulfilling orders and delivering ongoing customer support.
  • Improving our website performance and buyer experience.

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard data protection measures to keep your business communications confidential. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details with any third parties.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and visitor interactions and to improve our website. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage information. For more details, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please email us at info@akrmills.com.
