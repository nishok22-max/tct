About AKR Spinning Mills

A Family That Has Lived Cotton for Three Generations.

We are not a distant corporation — we are a family enterprise. Founded by N. Srinivasan as cotton traders and ginners, and developed into a premier spinning mill by MD Asok Kumar and JMD Keerthan Asok Kumar.

Our Mission

To produce consistent, high-performance 100% cotton, polyester-cotton, and blended yarns that knitters and weavers can rely on without compromise — backed by direct personal accountability.

Our Vision

To grow as a respected, family-run textile manufacturer — investing in our people, our machinery, and our community — while supplying yarn that meets the quality expectations of a competitive market.
