Chief Value Cotton (CVC)

Cotton Polyester Yarn (CP / CVC)

Our Cotton Polyester (CP / CVC) yarn is engineered for brands requiring the breathable softness of natural cotton with the enhanced tensile strength and shape retention of polyester. With cotton as the dominant fibre, this yarn offers supreme next-to-skin comfort.

Natural Touch & Synthetic Durability

The Best of Both Fibres

Cotton-Rich Comfort & Breathability

Formulated with 60% or more high-grade natural cotton, ensuring high moisture absorption, skin-friendly softness, and all-day cooling comfort.

Reduced Pilling & Low Shrinkage

The precision-blended polyester filament matrix significantly reduces fabric shrinkage and surface fuzzing across multiple domestic wash cycles.

Vibrant Colour Fastness

Excellent affinity for cross-dyeing and reactive dyes, yielding rich, enduring colours with outstanding wash and light fastness.

Smooth Knit & Weave Efficiency

Even drafting on modern ring frames guarantees uniform yarn mass, low hairiness, and seamless operation on high-speed circular knitting machines.
