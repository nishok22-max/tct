# Traders. Ginners. Spinners.
The key turning points that shaped our journey from cotton trading and ginning under N. Srinivasan to modern ring spinning under MD Asok Kumar and JMD Keerthan Asok Kumar.
1st Generation · Founder N. Srinivasan
## Raw Cotton Trading & Merchanting
Founding Era
N. Srinivasan establishes the family business in raw cotton trading. Direct procurement from growers and deep understanding of staple lengths establish the company's foundational reputation for fibre integrity.
1st to 2nd Generation Expansion
## Cotton Ginning Operations
Ginning Era
The family expands from trading into cotton ginning, separating seed cotton into clean lint bales. Controlling the ginning stage allows the family to ensure fibre purity and supply high-grade raw cotton directly to mills.
2nd Generation · Led by Asok Kumar (MD)
## Commissioning AKR Spinning Mills
Spinning Transition
Managing Director Asok Kumar leads the strategic leap into industrial manufacturing. AKR Spinning Mills is established with modern ring-spinning frames, carding lines, and autoconers from LMW, Trützschler, Rieter, and Saurer.
3rd Generation · Led by Keerthan Asok Kumar (JMD)
## Modernization, Blends & Combed Yarns
Current Era
Joint Managing Director Keerthan Asok Kumar leads technological modernization. The mill expands into 100% Combed Cotton, Polyester Cotton (PC), and Cotton Polyester (CP/CVC) blends, with laboratory testing benchmarked to international Uster standards.
Three Generations of Continuous Growth: From raw cotton merchanting to precision ring spinning, our family has dedicated over six decades to understanding cotton fibre from farm to yarn.
