# Three Generations of Stewardship.
AKR Spinning Mills is guided by three generations of hands-on family leadership, combining deep-rooted cotton trading heritage with modern ring-spinning technology.
Key Leadership
## The Family Behind AKR
From trading raw cotton bales to operating a world-class spinning fleet, our leaders have steered AKR with personal integrity and technical dedication.
11st Generation
Founding Visionary
### N. Srinivasan
Founder
Established the enterprise rooted in raw cotton merchanting and ginning. His foundational knowledge of cotton staple lengths, farmer relationships, and uncompromising trade integrity laid the bedrock of trust that AKR stands upon today.
Key Milestones & Focus:
Cotton Trading Roots
Ginning Expansion
Purity & Integrity
22nd Generation
Industrial Leadership
### Asok Kumar
Managing Director (MD)
Pioneered the family's transition from trading and ginning into modern ring spinning. He directed the establishment of our precision manufacturing infrastructure, commissioning advanced machinery from LMW, Trützschler, Rieter, and Saurer.
Key Milestones & Focus:
Spinning Mill Establishment
Machinery Modernization
Operational Excellence
33rd Generation
Next-Gen Growth & Technology
### Keerthan Asok Kumar
Joint Managing Director (JMD)
Directs current operations, quality benchmarking, and strategic expansion into synthetic-cotton blends (PC/CP) and high-end combed cotton yarns. Focused on automation, digital quality tracking, and global buyer relationships.
Key Milestones & Focus:
Product Diversification
Uster Quality Standards
Strategic Client Partnerships
Core Values
## What Our Leadership Brings
Our customers deal directly with decision-makers who have skin in the game.
### Cotton Knowledge
Three generations of hands-on expertise understanding cotton fibre from harvest and ginning to ring spinning.
### Hands-on Production Oversight
Leadership is actively present on the mill floor daily. Quality standards and technical tolerances are monitored personally.
### Direct Family Communication
No corporate bureaucracy or impersonal account reps. Buyers discuss specifications and commercial terms directly with the leadership.
### Quality Accountability
Quality is an inherited family reputation. Every shipment of 100% cotton, PC, and CP yarn is backed by our direct personal guarantee.
### Speak Directly With Our Leadership
Whether you need a specialized blend ratio, custom yarn counts, or scheduled export contracts, MD Asok Kumar and JMD Keerthan Asok Kumar are available to discuss your specifications personally.
