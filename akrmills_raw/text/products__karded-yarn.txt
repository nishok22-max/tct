Why Choose Karded Yarn
## The Benefits of Karded Cotton
### Natural Handle
Karded yarn retains a broader mix of fibre lengths than combed, giving it a slightly bulkier, softer feel — preferred for comfortable everyday garments and towels.
### Cost Efficiency
No combing step means less fibre waste (no noil removal) and a shorter process path — making karded yarn the economical choice for applications that don't require the premium finish of combing.
### Reliable Consistency
Even without combing, our carding and drawing stages are carefully managed to deliver a consistent count, controlled evenness, and minimal imperfection levels — lot after lot.
### Versatile Application
Karded yarn performs across a wide range of end products — from knitted garments and jerseys to towels and shirting fabrics — making it a versatile choice for manufacturers with varied production needs.
Natural Characteristics
## What Makes Karded Yarn What It Is
Karded yarn's character is shaped by the carding process itself — the stage that gives this yarn its name. Unlike combed yarn, where short fibres are removed by a combing cylinder, karded yarn retains a distribution of fibre lengths. This is not a flaw — it is a defining characteristic that makes karded yarn what it is.
In fabric, karded yarn produces a slightly softer, more textured surface than combed. For T-shirts, jerseys, and towels — where comfort and absorbency matter more than surface smoothness — these characteristics are an asset.
The slight hairiness of karded yarn also contributes to the softness that end-consumers expect in everyday cotton garments and household textiles.

Cost Efficiency
## Value Without Compromise
### No Combing Waste
Combing removes 12–20% of fibre as noil. Karded yarn uses the full card sliver — reducing raw material waste and keeping the process cost down.
### Shorter Process Path
Karded yarn skips the combing stage entirely. Fewer machines, fewer handling stages, and faster throughput all contribute to a leaner cost structure.
### Right Fit for Application
For everyday knitwear, jerseys, towels, and standard shirting, karded yarn delivers the required properties at a cost point that works commercially.

### Understanding Yarn Count (Ne)
Yarn count in the English (Ne) system indicates how many 840-yard hanks of yarn weigh one pound. A higher Ne number means a finer yarn — a 40s yarn is finer than a 20s yarn.
For karded yarn, the practical count range is typically in the coarser to medium segment of the market. Fine karded yarn is possible, but the natural variation from retained short fibres makes combed more suitable at very fine counts. We recommend discussing your count requirement with us directly.

Quality Assurance
## How We Ensure Quality at Every Lot
### Uster Benchmark Standards
Uster Technologies publishes annual statistics that benchmark yarn quality parameters — evenness, imperfections, hairiness — against global production averages. These statistics are the industry standard reference for yarn quality assessment.
We test our finished yarn on testing equipment that produces results comparable to Uster statistics. Quality positioning for each production lot can be discussed with buyers on enquiry. We do not publish specific Uster percentile claims without confirmed, current lot data.
### Parameters We Test
Count (Ne)Actual count vs. target count; CV% (Count Variation)
Tensile Strength (Tenacity)Force required to break the yarn, measured in cN/tex
Elongation at BreakHow much the yarn stretches before breaking
Evenness (U%)Measured by Uster tester; indicates mass variation in the yarn
ImperfectionsThin places, thick places, and neps per 1000 metres
HairinessThe fibre hairs extending from the yarn body — measured by Uster H value
### Lot-to-Lot Traceability
Each production lot is tracked through our process. Quality test results are maintained for each lot and can be made available to buyers as part of the supply relationship. Consistency across lots is a key part of how we approach quality management.
