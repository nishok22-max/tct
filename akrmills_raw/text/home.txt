## 100% Karded Cotton Yarn
Produced through our full ring-spinning line — from opening and carding through to winding and testing. Suited for knitting, jersey, shirting, and terry applications.

### Why Karded Yarn?
Karded yarn is produced without the combing step — retaining a mix of short and long fibres. This gives the yarn a natural, slightly softer handle and makes it cost-effective for a wide range of everyday textile applications.
-
Natural texture well-suited for knitted garments, jerseys, and shirts
-
Cost-effective without compromising on strength and evenness
-
Available in single and plied constructions (on enquiry)
-
Count range managed to your specification — enquire for availability
-
Quality benchmarked to Uster standards

How We Make It
## The Spinning Process
01
STEP 01
### Opening & Cleaning
Raw cotton is opened and cleaned to remove impurities and short fibres.
02
STEP 02
### Carding
Fibres are aligned into a continuous sliver; short fibres and neps are removed.
03
STEP 03
### Drawing
Multiple card slivers are combined and drawn to improve evenness.
04
STEP 04
### Roving
The drawn sliver is attenuated further and given a slight twist to prepare for spinning.
05
STEP 05
### Spinning
Roving is drafted and twisted into yarn on ring spinning frames.
06
STEP 06
### Winding
Yarn is wound onto cones and inspected for defects during transfer.
07
STEP 07
### Testing
Finished yarn is tested for count, strength, evenness, hairiness, and imperfections.
