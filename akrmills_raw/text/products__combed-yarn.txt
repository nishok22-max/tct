## The Combed Difference
### Silky Smooth Hand & Lustre
Mechanical combing removes short fibres and parallelizes long staple cotton, imparting a smooth, reflective surface and luxurious drape to finished fabrics.
### Minimal Hairiness (Low H-Value)
Reduced protruding fibres result in crisp stitch clarity in knitwear, smoother fabric handle, and significantly reduced pilling after prolonged wear.
### Superior Tensile Strength
Long, tightly interlocked cotton fibres provide high breaking strength and elongation, preventing yarn snapping on high-speed circular and warp knitting frames.
### Low Imperfections & Neps
Combing removes up to 18% of noils (short fibres, trash, and neps), ensuring ultra-clean yarn that produces flawless, streak-free fabrics when dyed.
