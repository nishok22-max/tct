Engineered Performance
## Why Choose AKR Polyester Cotton Yarn?
### Superior Tensile Strength
Virgin polyester staple fibres provide exceptional breaking tenacity (Rkm > 16.5), preventing warp breakage during high-speed weaving and circular knitting.
### Crease Recovery & Low Shrinkage
Synthetic component ensures garments resist wrinkling, dry quickly, and retain shape and dimensional stability after repeated commercial washes.
### Consistent Ring-Spun Evenness
Produced through precision blending in the blowroom and carding stages, ensuring uniform fibre distribution, low CV%, and minimal neps.
### Economical Performance
Combines the durability of polyester with the natural handle of cotton, delivering a cost-efficient yarn solution for mass apparel and uniform markets.
